## Research/design rationale from prior discussion
Learning-by-teaching and self-explanation motivate having learners explain concepts before a complete answer. Retrieval practice motivates recall before answer reveal. Work on guardrailed AI tutoring motivates protecting learner effort. Automated assessment research motivates rubrics, evidence spans, benchmark examples and explicit false-pass/false-fail testing. Child-centered AI design motivates data minimization, transparency, safety and age-appropriate interaction.

These rationales do not prove CogniFlip's educational efficacy. Do not claim learning impact until a suitable evaluation is conducted and actual evidence is cited.
